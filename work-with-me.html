Work With Me

Based in North York, Toronto; available for roles and projects across the GTA and remotely in Canada.

I take on a small number of projects alongside my day jobs, in three areas where this site already shows the work. Everything below links to proof, not promises.

Websites for small businesses

I design and build fast, hand-coded sites that small businesses can actually maintain. Recent work includes a shisha lounge site with menu and booking, a pharmacy website, a fruit shop, and a glass company site. This very site is the reference build: static, fast, accessible, and cheap to host.

Infrastructure and homelab builds

Proxmox virtualization, Kubernetes, storage, backups, DNS, and self-hosted services. I run a 7-node Kubernetes cluster, a zero-trust Proxmox cloud, and a physical server room, all documented end to end. I can help you plan, build, or document a similar setup, or untangle one that grew organically.

Security work

SOC-style alert triage and automation, Linux hardening, firewall and VLAN segmentation, and incident response runbooks. In past SOC work I cut alert MTTR by 25% and automated 30+ daily alerts. The security lab track shows how I work, step by step.

How it works

  1. Email me what you need: taki@tyfsadik.org.
  2. I reply with questions, a realistic scope, and whether I am the right person for it.
  3. If we proceed, you get written documentation of everything I build, the same standard as the project pages on this site.

For employment rather than projects, see my résumé and contact page.
