Who is Taki Sadik?

I build and defend infrastructure. From monitoring security events at a SOC to running self-hosted servers, private AI models, and recursive DNS, I care deeply about owning my stack.

Right now I work as an Incident Analyst at Shield Security Systems, triaging alerts and coordinating incident response, and as a Data Center Engineer (Internship) at Microsoft, applying cyber threat intelligence to physical infrastructure. I also serve as an Incident Manager at the CN Tower. Alongside all of that I am building Chakor, an open-source self-hosted AI workspace.

I got into IT through surveillance operations and discovered I cared more about the network underneath the cameras than the footage they captured. That curiosity led me to networking, then Linux administration, then cloud, then security. Today my home runs a 7-node bare-metal Kubernetes cluster, a zero-trust Proxmox cloud, and nine self-hosted services, from a private AI model to a public Wikipedia mirror.

I study at the University of Toronto (B.Eng, Information Technology) and Seneca Polytechnic (postgraduate, Cyber/Electronic Operations), and I document everything I learn as hands-on labs: 116 of them so far, each with real commands, diagrams, and measurable outcomes. I believe in learning by doing.
